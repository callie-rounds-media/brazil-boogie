## Answered, no action needed

**Brazilian federation paperwork for foreign jumpers: not required for this trip.**
CBPq Codigo Esportivo 2025, Art. 6 §1, says a foreign skydiver carries a licence issued by a body
CBPq recognises, which covers USPA, CSPA, APF and BPA. Art. 15 §1 says foreign licences are accepted
free of charge for up to 30 days, and only past that does anyone have to register with CBPq. The
trip runs 8 days, so nobody needs Brazilian paperwork.

Art. 6 also requires every jumper to carry a logbook, the Caderneta de Saltos, alongside the licence.
Worth a line in the email so people pack both.
