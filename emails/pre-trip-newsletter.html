Búzios is close
Five weeks to go. This is the first of a few short letters to get you ready for the trip, so here is everything to sort before you fly. It should take you an evening.
VISA
Apply this week
US, Canadian and Australian passports need an e-visa for Brazil. It is done online, costs US$80.90, and usually comes back within a few days, though it can take up to ten business days. Apply only through the official portal, and check your passport has six months left past the trip.
APPLY FOR THE E-VISA
FLIGHTS
Fly into Rio
Book into Rio de Janeiro Galeão, airport code GIG. Land on Monday 12 October and fly home on or after Monday 19 October. Búzios is a scenic drive up the coast from there, and we take care of that part.
ARRIVAL
How you will be met
A driver will be waiting for you in arrivals and will take you straight to the villa. Transfers are included both ways, so there is nothing to arrange. On the last day the driver brings you back to the airport in time for your flight.
ONE THING TO DO
Send us your flights
Once you have booked, reply to this email with your airline, flight numbers and arrival and departure times. We need them no later than 5 October, a week before the trip, so we can arrange your driver. Anything that arrives after that may mean organising your own ride to the villa.
