Your Brazil trip is just over a month away and we could not be more excited to welcome you! There are a few things to sort before you fly, so please read through this email to make sure you are ready for the trip :)
 
YOUR TO-DO LIST
Visa, flights, gear and insurance
1. Apply for your Brazil e-visa this week
US, Canadian and Australian passports need one. It is done online for US$80.90 and usually comes back within a few days, up to ten business days when busy. Use only the official portal, brazil.vfsevisa.com, and check your passport has six months left past the trip.
2. Book flights into Rio Galeão (GIG) or Cabo Frio (CFB)
Aim to land on Monday 12 October at either Rio Galeão (GIG) or Cabo Frio (CFB). GIG is the one to book for international flights and is three hours by road from the villa. Cabo Frio is only thirty minutes away but takes domestic flights only, so you would connect through São Paulo. Either way, a driver will meet you in arrivals and take you straight to the villa, and airport transfers are covered for anyone leaving on the 19th.
3. Send us your flight details by 5 October
Reply to this email with the airport you are flying into, your airline and flight numbers, and your arrival and departure times. We need them a week before the trip to arrange your driver. Anything after that may mean organising your own ride to the villa.
4. Check your licence and your reserve
A reminder that you need a USPA B licence for the week, so bring your logbook and make sure your reserve is in date.
5. Get insurance that covers skydiving
Check that your policy covers skydiving, and if it does not, grab temporary cover for the trip so you are sorted.
6. Travelling with your rig
Bring it as carry-on in its own bag and give yourself an extra half hour at the airport. Worth printing the CYPRES x-ray card (or the Vigil one) and the TSA parachute page in case anyone asks, but most rigs sail straight through.
