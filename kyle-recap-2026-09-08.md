# Brazil Boogie, where the pre-trip email stands

For Kyle, 8 September 2026.

## Answering your questions

**No Brazilian federation paperwork is needed.** CBPq's own Codigo Esportivo 2025 settles it. Article 6
says a foreign jumper carries a licence issued by a body CBPq recognises, which covers USPA. Article 15
says foreign licences are accepted free of charge for up to 30 days, and only past that does anyone have
to register with CBPq. The trip runs eight days, so nobody needs to file anything.

The same article does require every jumper to carry a logbook alongside the licence, so that is now in
the email.

**The waiver cannot be Skydive Búzios' tandem form.** Their form releases Skydive Búzios for tandem jumps.
It does not name the LLC and it says nothing about the helicopter. Their form also will not give us media
rights, which is the piece we actually need signed.

**Insurance is only on the jumping.** An operator's policy covers that operator, so the one thing worth
having is our LLC named as an additional insured on the policies Skydive Búzios and the helicopter company
already carry, which is a request to them rather than something we buy. The villa, the vans, the beach days
and the group outings are deliberately left alone.

**Food, dishes and house rules are staying out of the email.** Callie is covering all of it at the welcome
dinner on the first night, where it lands better anyway. The one exception is a single line saying meals
are not included, because people are budgeting for the week right now.

**Guests sign in person on arrival.** No online form tool, no chasing anyone by email.

**Packing list and the money and connectivity section are cut.** They made the email long without changing
what anyone actually has to do before flying.
