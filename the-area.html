The area

Geribá Beach  ·  Búzios, Brazil

Getting there

A private van meets your flight, whatever time it lands, and drives you to the villa. The same is arranged for your departure.

Before you fly

US, Canadian and Australian passport holders need a Brazilian eVisa. It is applied for online, costs around 80 US dollars, and takes about five working days to come back. Do it as soon as you book, not the week before you fly.

The town

Rua das Pedras is six hundred metres of pedestrian street along the seafront. Quiet by day, then the centre of the peninsula's nightlife after dark. Bars, galleries, ice cream, and clubs that run late.

Eating

Búzios takes food seriously. Bar do Zé and Fishbone for seafood, Sushigeriba for Japanese, Altto for Italian, 74 Osteria for a tasting menu on the sea, Chez Pitu down on the sand.

Your beach

The villa sits above Geribá. Two kilometres of white sand, kiosks the length of it, surfable waves at one end and paddleboards for hire. It is the beach at the bottom of your hill.

The pink beach

Praia Rosa, a short ride around the headland, where the sand really does run pink at the waterline. Quiet, shallow and clear, and most people who come to Búzios never find it.

The other beaches

Ferradura is a horseshoe bay a few minutes away, flat and calm, good for swimming and snorkelling. João Fernandes has the clearest water of any of them.

If you have never been to Brazil

Búzios is a resort town, not a city. It is where Rio goes on holiday. Espresso, card machines, English on menus, and a villa rated 4.91 by the people who have stayed in it.
