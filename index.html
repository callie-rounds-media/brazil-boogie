Skydiving above Brazil

This October we take over a villa on the cliffs of Brazil, filling it with people who want the same week.

We'll run helicopter skydives through the day and into sunset, over one of the most spectacular coastlines in the world.

Down the hill is Búzios itself. White sand beaches, surfing, and a town that does not go quiet after dark, with bars and restaurants running right along the water.
